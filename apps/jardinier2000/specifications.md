# Specifications

Here is a list of requirements that the jardinier2000 is intended to fulfill. This should be used as the guideline to ensure the development of the project is going toward the right direction.

- The design must be water proof (at least IP65):
  - 6: Protected against access to hazardous parts. Dust-tight.
  - 5: Protected against water jets from any angle.

## Connectivity

- 4 ports available.
- Each port provides:
  - 2-way communication using RS485 transport.
  - 5V power supply, 1A max per port.
- The esp32s3 is the master
- Everything connected to the ports are slaves.
